ShiftSum Privacy Policy

ShiftSum Shift & Earnings Log · Effective 4 October 2026

ShiftSum is a shift and earnings log for hourly workers. It is designed to work entirely offline. We do not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

Data you enter

Everything you record in ShiftSum — jobs, pay rates, shifts, hours, tips, notes and settings — is stored only in a local database on your device. It is never sent to us or to any third party. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it (unless you exported a backup yourself).

Backups and exports

You can export your data as a backup or CSV file. These files are created on your device and go only where you choose to send them (for example, to your own cloud storage or email). We have no access to them.

App lock and biometrics

If you turn on App Lock, ShiftSum asks the operating system (Face ID, Touch ID or your device's biometric/passcode service) to confirm it is you. ShiftSum never sees or stores your biometric data; the check is performed entirely by iOS or Android.

Notifications

Reminders (for example, to end a running shift) are scheduled locally on your device. No notification service of ours is involved.

Purchases

The optional one-time Pro unlock is processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We receive no payment details or personal information from that transaction; Apple's and Google's own privacy policies apply to the payment itself.

Home-screen widget

The widget shows a summary of your current week and running shift. This data is shared only between the app and its widget on your device.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

ShiftSum contains no analytics, no advertising SDKs and no tracking. The app makes no network requests of its own.

Children

ShiftSum is a general-audience productivity tool and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes
